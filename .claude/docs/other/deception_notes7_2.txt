Datasets:
Sycophancy
nrimsky/CAA (github.com/nrimsky/CAA): ready-formatted contrastive pairs (1000 generation pairs + 50 test) already structured for difference-in-means extraction. This is what most steering work builds on.
Anthropic/model-written-evals (HF, sycophancy/ folder): the original GPT-4-generated sycophancy eval set that CAA's pairs derive from. Free, larger, but you'll format it yourself.
Sandbagging
Pre-made password-locked models: skip fine-tuning entirely. These models will sandbag when a password is included in the prompt. FelixHofstaetter/mistral-7b-sandbagging-new and redwoodresearch/math_pwd_lock_deepseek_math7b on HF. The "standard" condition includes the password; the sandbagging condition omits it — that locked-vs-elicited pair is your contrastive signal.
TeunvdWeij/sandbagging (github.com/TeunvdWeij/sandbagging) — the original paper's ("AI Sandbagging: Language Models can Strategically Underperform on Evaluations" (van der Weij, Hofstätter, Jaffe, Brown & Ward, arXiv 2406.07358)) repo, with synthetic dataset generation and password-locked training scripts on WMDP. 
cais/wmdp (HF): the underlying hazardous-knowledge MCQ benchmark the sandbagging is measured on.
Gemma caveat: the password-locked models are Mistral/Llama. To get sandbagging on Gemma 2 2B, your cheapest route is prompt-based sandbagging (system prompt instructing underperformance) rather than fine-tuning your own locked Gemma. Note that prompt-based elicitation is reportedly less reliable than training-based, so verify the behavior actually appears before trusting the direction.
Concealment / omission
ApolloResearch/deception-detection (github.com/ApolloResearch/deception-detection): it bundles the insider-trading concealment scenario (Scheurer et al.), roleplay data, sandbagging data, and the REPE honest/dishonest instruction pairs. In the insider-trading scenario the model trades on a tip against policy, is then asked whether it used insider information, and gets an opportunity to lie: that's concealment with a clean ground-truth label.
REPE honesty pairs (Zou et al. 2023) — 612 contrastive prompt pairs where only the "honest AI" vs "dishonest AI" instruction differs while the response is held identical. Bundled in the Apollo repo. This is your general deception direction baseline
DolusChat (HF: AlignmentResearch/DolusChat) — broad, with controllable deception variables.
Liars' Bench (2511.16035) — use for held-out evaluation, not training. It spans multiple deception types including instructed deception, convincing-game, and insider trading, and is the right place to stress-test whether your directions generalize.
Benchmarks:
MASK: Model Alignment between Statements and Knowledge, a benchmark for measuring lies of commission in LLMs 
evaluation pipeline that directly measures when models lie by eliciting their underlying beliefs and testing whether models will contradict those beliefs when pressured to lie
