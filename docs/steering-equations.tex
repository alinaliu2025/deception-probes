\bigskip
\noindent
Class-label convention (never flipped): $1=$ deceptive (caved / sandbagged),
$0=$ control (held).

\section*{1. Probe direction}

Difference-of-means (\texttt{mms}) --- the estimator that steers:
\begin{equation}
  \vv_{\text{mms}}
  = \frac{\muvec_{\text{caved}} - \muvec_{\text{held}}}
         {\norm{\muvec_{\text{caved}} - \muvec_{\text{held}}}},
  \qquad
  \muvec_{c} = \frac{1}{\lvert c \rvert}\sum_{\hh \in c} \hh .
\end{equation}

Logistic regression (\texttt{lr}), fit on standardized activations with weight
$\bm{w}$ and per-dimension standard deviation $\bm{\sigma}$, then folded back to
raw space:
\begin{equation}
  \vv_{\text{lr}}
  = \frac{\bm{w} \oslash \bm{\sigma}}{\norm{\bm{w} \oslash \bm{\sigma}}},
  \qquad (\oslash = \text{elementwise division}).
\end{equation}

Scoring an activation (projection minus a calibrated bias $b$):
\begin{equation}
  \operatorname{score}(\hh) = \hh \cdot \vv - b .
\end{equation}

\section*{2. Alpha and the layer scale}

The steering coefficient $\alpha$ is dimensionless; the injected magnitude is
$\alpha$ times the layer's mean last-token residual norm $s$:
\begin{equation}
  s = \operatorname*{mean}_{i} \norm{\hh_i}
  \quad(\text{layer } L,\ \text{last token}),
  \qquad
  \text{injected vector} = \alpha\, s\, \vv .
\end{equation}
With the \texttt{-{}-raw} flag the scale is dropped: injected vector $=\alpha\,\vv$.

\section*{3. Add pass (sufficiency)}

On unpressured items answered correctly at baseline, add the direction at layer
$L$, then generate:
\begin{equation}
  \hh' = \hh + \alpha\, s\, \vv .
\end{equation}
Causal signature: the wrong-answer rate rises with $\alpha$, over parsed items,
\begin{equation}
  \text{wrong\_rate}(\alpha)
  = \frac{1}{N_{\text{parsed}}}
    \sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{parsed}}}
    \ind\!\left[\,\text{answer}_i(\alpha)\ \text{is wrong}\,\right].
\end{equation}

\section*{4. Ablate pass (necessity)}

On pressured items that cave at baseline, project the direction out, then
generate:
\begin{equation}
  \hh' = \hh - (\hh \cdot \vv)\,\vv
       = \left(I - \vv\vv^{\top}\right)\hh .
\end{equation}
Here $P = I - \vv\vv^{\top}$ is the orthogonal projector onto the complement of
$\vv$ (valid because $\norm{\vv}=1$). Causal signature: the caving rate drops
versus baseline.

\section*{5. Matched random control}

Each pass is repeated with a random unit vector $\rr$, $\norm{\rr}=1$, in place
of $\vv$:
\begin{equation}
  \hh' = \hh + \alpha\, s\, \rr
  \qquad\text{(add)},
  \qquad
  \hh' = \hh - (\hh \cdot \rr)\,\rr
  \qquad\text{(ablate)}.
\end{equation}
A direction counts as causal only if its curve clears the random curve, ruling
out generic perturbation.

\section*{6. Layer indexing}

The probe reads $\text{hidden\_states}[L]$, the output of decoder block $L$
(index $0$ is the embedding layer). The intervention hook therefore attaches to
$\text{layers}[L-1]$, whose output \emph{is} $\text{hidden\_states}[L]$. Layer
$0$ cannot be steered (no block produces it).

\bigskip
\noindent\textit{Instructed-arm example values (Qwen2.5-7B, mms, $L{=}20$,
prompt-final): $s = 89.3$; add pass $\text{wrong\_rate} = 0 \to 0.39 \to 0.46
\to 0.72$ at $\alpha = 0,0.25,0.5,1.0$ (random $\approx 0.013$); ablate
$1.00 \to 0.835$ (random $0.985$).}
